Culture & Rules Comparison

A deep dive into how discipline and tradition shape the student experience.

Local Schools: Tradition & Discipline

  • Strict adherence to national identity and cultural values.
  • High emphasis on discipline and standardized uniforms.
  • Rigid rules regarding hair, appearance, and conduct.
  • Structured hierarchy between teachers and students.

International Schools: Diversity & Freedom

  • Diverse environments with a global perspective.
  • More freedom in personal expression and style.
  • Flexible rules that prioritize individual growth.
  • Open dialogue and collaborative teacher-student bonds.

Support & Teamwork

Local students often face more challenges and are more likely to seek help from peers and mentors. This fosters a strong sense of community and traditional teamwork.

Local Focus Community

Independence & Tasks

International students are encouraged to be more independent. Their collaboration is often task-based, focusing on efficiency and individual responsibility within a group.
